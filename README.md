## Why Markdown?

- **Instantly searchable** across the entire Bible
- **Works offline** with AI assistants
- **Easy to embed** in study tools, curriculum, and presentations
- **Human-readable** source — no app or API needed
- **Standard format** — works with any text editor or AI platform

## How to Use

Place any translation folder in your agent's workspace:

```
~/.openclaw/workspace/bibles/English/bsb/bsb.md
```

Then ask your assistant questions like:
- "What does BSB say about faith?"
- "Read Genesis 1:1-5 from BSB"
- "Find all verses about grace"
- "Compare ESV and KJV for Romans 8:28"

## Format

All translations use consistent Markdown formatting:
- `# Book Name` for book headers
- `## Chapter N` for chapter headers
- `**vN**` for verse markers

- **[Chinese CUV](Chinese/CUV/README.md):** Chinese Union Version, Traditional old-punctuation 神-edition family (public domain); resolved the Traditional-FULL vs Simplified-book divergence by regenerating all 66 books to match the Traditional FULL; 116 Psalm superscriptions separated, 12 public-domain section headings added, Luke 12:19 bracket artifact removed, 64 combined ranges preserved. All 1,189 chapters compared against the archived Traditional witness with **zero unexplained differences**; 27 tests pass.

- **[English BSB](English/bsb/README.md):** Berean Standard Bible, **public domain (CC0**, dedicated April 30, 2023). These are high-confidence source-fidelity checks, not guarantees of infallibility or independent scholarly collations. Per-translation residual gaps are documented in each README/audit report.

### Other files

**Do not treat the remaining collection as verified.** Recent spot checks found wrong-language assignments, truncated verses, footnote contamination and other extraction defects. ESV, the other French translations and Russian repairs are still in progress; incomplete staged candidates have not replaced their published files. Historical audit files outside the accepted rebuilds may be stale.

Combined verse ranges, Psalm superscriptions and edition-specific canon/numbering must be evaluated against the actual source, not a universal verse-count template.
